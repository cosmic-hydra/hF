How we collect, use and protect your personal information.

Last updated: July 2026. This policy explains how h7d LLC (“hydra fund”, “we”, “us”) handles personal information collected through this website.

What we collect

When you submit a form on this site (contact or newsletter), we collect the information you provide — typically your name, email address and message. Our forms are processed by FormSubmit, which forwards your submission to us by email. We do not run analytics trackers or advertising cookies on this site.

How we use it

We use your information to respond to your enquiry, send you the updates you asked for, and operate our business. We do not sell your personal information. We may share it with service providers who help us operate (such as email and form processing), or where required by law.

Retention and your choices

We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purposes above. You can unsubscribe from our newsletter at any time, and you can ask us to access, correct or delete your information by emailing advaith@hydrafund.ch.

Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be published on this page.
