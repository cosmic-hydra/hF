Building the world’s first AI-native fund

Every investment firm now claims to “use AI.” A model here, a chatbot there, a slide in the pitch deck. But bolting intelligence onto a firm designed in the 1980s is like strapping a jet engine to a horse cart — the constraint was never the engine.

We started hydra fund from a different premise: what would an investment firm look like if it were designed, from the first line of code, around artificial intelligence?

Not a tool. A substrate.

In an AI-native firm, models don’t assist the process — they are the process. Sourcing, research, underwriting, risk monitoring and reporting all run continuously, in parallel, at machine speed. Humans sit where they belong: at the top of the loop, exercising judgment, setting objectives and making the final call.

AI proposes. People dispose. That ordering is the entire design.

Why one firm, five disciplines

The traditional industry splits capital across silos — a hedge fund here, a venture fund there, a bank and a consultancy somewhere else, each with its own fees, incentives and blind spots. Intelligence compounds when information flows. So we fused five disciplines into one platform: hedge fund, venture capital, startup accelerator, investment banking and consultancy.

What our public-markets engine learns about a sector flows into how we underwrite startups in it. What our accelerator sees on the ground flows back into how we trade the incumbents. One relationship, one intelligence, compounding in every direction.

What this means for you

For our clients, AI-native isn’t a buzzword — it’s a service level. It means your portfolio is watched around the clock, your questions are answered with live data instead of last quarter’s PDF, and your capital gets access to opportunities that used to require an institution’s scale.

We’re at the very beginning. If you’d like to be part of it — as a client, a founder or a teammate — we’re ready to listen.
