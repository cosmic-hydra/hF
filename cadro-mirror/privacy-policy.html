Privacy Policy

Introduction

Last updated: 20 July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how h7d LLC, trading as hydra fund (“hydra fund”, “we”, “us” or “our”), a Delaware limited liability company with its registered address at 8 The Green, Ste A, Dover, Delaware 19901, United States, collects, uses, stores, shares and protects personal information when you visit hydrafund.ch or www.hydrafund.ch (the “Website”), sign up to our newsletter, contact us, or otherwise interact with us. We are the data controller of the personal information described in this policy.

Although hydra fund is not based in Switzerland and uses the .ch domain solely to indicate the Swiss nature and quality aspirations of the company, we voluntarily seek to handle personal data in a manner consistent with high international data protection standards, including principles reflected in the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP) and the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), in addition to applicable U.S. law.

By using our website, mobile app or services, you confirm you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

Information We Collect About You

We collect and process the following categories of information:

  • Information you give us. Name, email address and any other details you provide when you sign up to our newsletter, complete a contact form, or correspond with us by email or otherwise, including the content of your messages.
  • Technical information collected automatically. IP address, browser type and version, device type, operating system, time-zone setting, language, referring website, and pages visited, collected through server logs, cookies and similar technologies.
  • Usage information. How you navigate and interact with the Website, including clicks, scrolling and time spent on pages.

We do not intentionally collect special categories of personal data (such as health, religious or biometric data), and we ask that you do not send us such data. The Website is not directed at children under 18 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

We use your information to: operate, secure and improve the Website; respond to your enquiries; send you newsletters and updates you have requested; comply with legal obligations; and establish, exercise or defend legal claims. Where GDPR-style rules apply, our legal bases are your consent, our legitimate interests in operating and promoting hydra fund, and compliance with legal obligations.

Cookies

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. We use the following categories of cookies, as described in our cookie banner:

  • Strictly necessary cookies — required for basic Website functionality and security. These are always active.
  • Analytics cookies — help us understand how the Website performs and how visitors interact with it.
  • Marketing cookies — used to deliver advertising more relevant to you and your interests.
  • Personalization cookies — allow the Website to remember choices you make, such as language or region.

You can manage your preferences at any time through the cookie preferences panel on the Website, and you can block or delete cookies through your browser settings. If you disable cookies, some parts of the Website may not function properly.

Session Recordings and Analytics

We may use analytics and session-replay tools to understand how visitors use the Website, diagnose technical issues and improve the user experience. These tools may record aggregated or pseudonymised information about page views, mouse movements, clicks and scrolling. Where such tools are used, we configure them to avoid capturing sensitive input wherever possible, and we use the resulting data only for analytics, security and product-improvement purposes. You can object to analytics processing by adjusting your cookie preferences or contacting us at advaith@hydrafund.ch.

Who We Give Your Information To

We do not sell your personal information. We may share your information with:

  • Service providers acting on our behalf, such as website hosting (including GitHub Pages), form-processing and email providers, and analytics providers, in each case only to the extent necessary for them to perform their services;
  • Professional advisers such as lawyers, accountants and auditors, where reasonably necessary;
  • Authorities and regulators where we are required to do so by law, court order or legal process, or to protect our rights, property or safety or those of others;
  • Successors in title in connection with any merger, acquisition, reorganisation or sale of assets, in which case your information may be transferred as part of that transaction subject to this policy.

Where We Store Your Information

Your information is stored on servers operated by our service providers, which may be located in the United States, the European Economic Area, Switzerland, the United Kingdom or other jurisdictions. Where your information is transferred outside your home jurisdiction, we take reasonable steps to ensure it receives an adequate level of protection, including using providers that offer appropriate contractual and technical safeguards. By using the Website you acknowledge that your information may be transferred to and processed in the United States and other countries whose data protection laws may differ from those of your jurisdiction.

How Long We Keep Your Information

We keep your personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes for which it was collected, including to satisfy any legal, accounting or reporting requirements. In general: newsletter data is kept until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it; enquiry correspondence is kept for as long as needed to handle the enquiry and for a reasonable period thereafter; and technical logs are kept for a limited period for security and diagnostic purposes. When information is no longer needed, we delete it or irreversibly anonymise it.

Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have some or all of the following rights in relation to your personal information:

  • the right to access the personal information we hold about you;
  • the right to have inaccurate or incomplete information corrected;
  • the right to have your information deleted (“right to be forgotten”);
  • the right to restrict or object to our processing of your information;
  • the right to receive your information in a portable format;
  • the right to withdraw consent at any time, where processing is based on consent; and
  • the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory or data protection authority in your jurisdiction.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at advaith@hydrafund.ch. We may need to verify your identity before acting on your request. We will respond within any timescale required by applicable law.

Marketing and Communications

Where you have signed up to our newsletter or otherwise consented, we may send you news, insights and updates about hydra fund. Every marketing email we send includes a way to opt out, and you may unsubscribe at any time by following the instructions in the message or by emailing advaith@hydrafund.ch. Opting out of marketing does not affect service or administrative communications that we may need to send you.

Security

We implement reasonable technical and organisational measures designed to protect your personal information against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, loss or destruction, including encrypted connections (HTTPS), access controls and reputable hosting providers. However, no method of transmission over the internet or method of electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. You transmit information to us at your own risk. If we become aware of a personal data breach affecting you, we will notify you and any relevant authority where required by applicable law.

Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns or complaints about this Privacy Policy or our handling of your personal information, or wish to exercise any of your rights, please contact us at advaith@hydrafund.ch or write to h7d LLC, 8 The Green, Ste A, Dover, Delaware 19901, United States. We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time; the “Last updated” date above indicates when it was last revised, and your continued use of the Website after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
