Our mission

Our purpose is to build the world’s first AI-native fund — fusing a hedge fund, investment bank, startup accelerator, VC fund and consultancy into one intelligent firm, so our clients can compound capital and back what’s next with confidence.
We’re here to reinvent finance with AI at its core — so whether you’re working with our investment team, our engineers or our operators, you can be confident the full intelligence of hydra fund is working for you, whenever you need.
